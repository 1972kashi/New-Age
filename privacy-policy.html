Privacy Policy

This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Personal data you provide directly: name, email, phone, and enquiry details.
  • Account data used for login and profile management.
  • Technical data from your browser and device, such as IP address and device type.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide the service, respond to enquiries, improve the site, and keep your account secure.

3. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with service providers that help operate the website, such as hosting and analytics providers.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as needed to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

  • Access and correct personal information.
  • Request deletion of your account data.
  • Opt out of marketing communications.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to keep your data secure.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with the effective date.

8. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, contact our support team through the website.
